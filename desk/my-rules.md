# Paper Trading Desk Rules

MODE: PAPER ONLY
PAPER BALANCE: $10000
WATCHLIST: NVDA, AAPL, MSFT, AMD, META, TSLA, SPY, BTC, ETH
MAX SIZE: 5% of the balance per paper trade
MAX LOSS: 1% of the balance per trade
DAILY LIMIT: 2% loss per day triggers pause
ALWAYS SKIP: memecoins, coins less than 30 days old, stocks under $5, anything without a real source
CARDS: 3 per day, maximum
CHANGES: only the human can change this file
